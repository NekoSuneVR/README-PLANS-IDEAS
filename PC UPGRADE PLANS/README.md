# PSU

## New PC: 850W

Keep the new build at **850W**.

The RTX 3060 does not require 850W, but this gives room for a future GPU upgrade.

Prefer:

- 850W
- 80+ Gold
- ATX 3.0 or ATX 3.1
- PCIe 5 / 12V-2x6 where possible
- Reputable manufacturer

# Purchase Strategy

1. Buy the Ryzen 5 5600 and B550 motherboard.
2. Hunt for a tested RTX 3060 12GB around £200–£230.
3. Buy 32GB DDR4.
4. Buy a 1TB NVMe SSD.
5. Buy a quality 850W PSU.
6. Build everything in the MONTECH XR.
7. Leave the current i7-6700K PC completely intact.
8. Later convert the old PC into the server.
9. Optionally replace the GTX 980 Ti with a GTX 1660 SUPER for transcoding.
